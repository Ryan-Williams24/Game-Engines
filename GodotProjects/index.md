## Meadow Hop

A 2D platformer with coins, patrolling slimes, checkpoints, and a finish flag.

<a href="MeadowHop.zip" download>Download Meadow Hop — Godot project (.zip)</a>

### How to play

1. Unzip `MeadowHop.zip`.
2. In Godot 4.3 or newer, choose **Import** and select `MeadowHop/project.godot`.
3. Open the project and press **F5**.
4. Press **Enter** or click **Let's play** to start.

Move with **A/D** or the **arrow keys**, jump with **Space**, pause with **Escape**, and restart with **R**.

This download contains the editable Godot project and requires the Godot editor to run.
