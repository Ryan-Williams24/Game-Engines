# Meadow Hop

A complete, small 2D platformer for **Godot 4.3 or newer**. All artwork and sound effects are generated by the project; there are no external assets, downloads, plugins, or C# dependencies.

## Play

1. Open Godot and click **Import**.
2. Select `project.godot` in this folder and open the project.
3. Press **F5** (or the Play button), then click **Let's play** or press **Enter**.
4. Reach the yellow finish flag at the far right of the meadow.

| Control | Action |
| --- | --- |
| A / D or Left / Right | Move |
| Space / W / Up | Jump (hold for a higher jump) |
| Escape / P | Pause / resume |
| R | Restart the whole game |
| Enter | Start / resume / play again |

## What's included

- A scrolling level with optional platforms, pits, and spikes.
- Eight patrolling slimes. Jump onto them from above to defeat them and bounce; touching their sides costs a heart.
- Three hearts and three lives at the start. Losing all hearts or falling into a pit costs one life. Respawning restores all hearts.
- Brief protection and knockback after damage, plus protection after respawning.
- Coins worth 10 points; defeated slimes are worth 100 points. Every 15 coins earns an extra life.
- Three heart pickups that restore one heart. They stay available while health is full.
- A white checkpoint flag halfway through the level that turns green when activated. You keep collected coins and defeated enemies when you lose a life.
- Title, pause, victory, and game-over menus with clickable buttons and keyboard controls.
- Generated sound effects, animated characters and coins, and a HUD.
- Buffered jumps and a short grace period when stepping off a platform.

## Edit the game

- `scripts/player.gd`: movement speed, jump height, gravity, and player drawing.
- `scripts/enemy.gd`: slime movement and drawing.
- `scripts/game.gd`: level rectangles, enemy patrols, pickups, health/lives, checkpoint, interface, sounds, and game rules.
- `scenes/main.tscn`: the main scene. Its script builds the level at runtime.

To add terrain, add a `Rect2(x, y, width, height)` to `PLATFORMS` in `game.gd`. To add a ground enemy, add a `Vector3(left_edge, right_edge, speed)` to `PATROLS`; keep its entire patrol on solid ground and away from spikes.

## Validation

With a Godot executable on your PATH:

```sh
godot --headless --path . --editor --quit
godot --headless --fixed-fps 60 --path . --script res://tests/gameplay_test.gd
```

The integration checks load the real main scene and test movement, jumping, stomping, damage protection, health pickups, coins and extra lives, checkpoint respawning, pits, pause, game over, victory, and a clean restart.

Verified in Godot 4.7.2 on October 7, 2026: **24 checks passed**, including a complete level traversal using movement and jump input.
