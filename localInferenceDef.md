# Local Inference Definition

**Local Inference** is the practice of running an Artificial Intelligence (AI) model—like a Large Language Model (LLM)—**directly on your own device** (your personal computer, laptop, or mobile phone) instead of sending your data and prompts to a remote server (the cloud) to be processed.

### Key Concepts:

*   **Execution Location:** The model runs on your local hardware (your device).
*   **Data Flow:** Your prompts and data stay on your computer; they do not travel over the internet to a remote data center.
*   **Benefits:**
    *   **Privacy & Security:** Maximizes data privacy as sensitive information never leaves your device.
    *   **Speed:** Provides very fast response times (low latency) because there is no network delay.
    *   **No Dependency:** You are not reliant on external service providers.

**In short:** Local Inference means running the AI model *on-device* rather than relying on a remote cloud service.
